Why operators choose it

Built for the people who answer to CBP

Zone work is unforgiving: one wrong balance, one missed deadline, one unexplained adjustment. FTZ Control is designed so those problems are stopped at the keyboard, not found in an audit.

One ledger, exact to the last decimal

Every lot traces from its e214 line to its last withdrawal. Commercial and customs units move together, so partial shipments never leave a rounding gap.

FIFO that enforces itself

Oldest stock goes first. An out-of-order withdrawal is refused with the lot you should use, and one click splits a withdrawal across lots for you.

Problems caught before filing

Each e214 is checked against your WMS receiving log and product master before it is submitted. A daily reconciliation flags overages, shortages and receipts nobody admitted.

In-bond without the sweat

IT, TE and IE movements with due dates, late-arrival flags and proof of closure, so no bond stays open by accident.

A history nobody can quietly edit

The audit log and stock ledger are append-only and fingerprinted. Verify the whole chain in one click, and note the fingerprint outside the system.

Type it once

Your product master fills HTSUS, origin, units and duty rate. 3461 / 7501 data worksheets come straight from the ledger, ready for your broker to file.

On every screen you use

Install it on a desktop, tablet or phone. One live ledger, the same numbers everywhere.

How it works

From inbound truck to audit-ready, in three moves

  1. ConnectSend parts, receipts and counts from your ERP or WMS, or import a CSV. Nothing to re-key.
  2. OperateAdmit with e214, process under e216, ship by consumption, export, transfer or in-bond. Every step is validated as you go.
  3. Stay audit-readyDaily reconciliation, integrity checks and entry worksheets are always one click away.

Connections

Connect the ERP you already run

Your master ERP stays the system of record for orders, stock and finance. FTZ Control sits beside it: your systems push parts, receipts and counts in, and the zone ledger your auditors need comes back out.

1. Get a feed-only key

Your administrator issues a secure API key per client. It can send data in; it cannot approve, withdraw or read anything else.

2. Send your data

Post JSON from your ERP or WMS, or drop in a CSV, for parts, receiving logs and physical counts.

3. See mismatches early

Differences between what your systems say and what the zone ledger says surface before they reach CBP.
