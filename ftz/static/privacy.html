Privacy notice

What FTZ Control stores and why. Last updated 29 October 2026.

What is stored

  • Account details: user name, optional email address, role, a password hash, the time of the last sign-in, and, if you turn on two-factor sign-in, the authenticator key and hashed recovery codes.
  • Zone records you enter or your systems send: admissions, permits, in-bond movements, parts, inventory, filings, supporting documents, and the audit log of who did what and when.

Why

To run your foreign-trade zone or bonded warehouse and to give you a record you can show customs and your auditors. Nothing is used for advertising or sold.

Who can see it

  • People you create accounts for, within the permissions of their role.
  • The hosting providers that run the app and the database, as processors of the data, under their own security commitments.
  • No one else, unless you share an export or the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Trade and customs records are kept until your organization decides to remove them. Customs rules generally require records to be kept for several years, so documents and history cannot be deleted from inside the app: documents are voided, and the audit log is permanent. Administrators can disable a user at any time.

Your choices

  • Administrators can download a complete copy of the data (Audit and Export) and export any ledger as CSV.
  • Ask your administrator to correct or remove an account, or to reset two-factor sign-in.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. It is HttpOnly, is not used for tracking, and is removed when you sign out or it expires. The only third-party request is the Google Fonts stylesheet used for the typeface.
